# Onitama sounds

All six sounds are synthesized in-repo by `tools/onitama-art/make_sounds.py`. They use no samples and no third-party audio, so no licence applies.

- `wood-clack`: piece placed
- `slide`: piece sliding
- `capture`: heavier soft thock
- `card-swish`: card moving
- `bell`: soft temple bell for a win
- `turn-tick`: light wood tap

Every sound peaks at about -14 dBFS, with rounded attacks and a low-pass filter.
